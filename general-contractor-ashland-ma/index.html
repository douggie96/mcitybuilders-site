General contractor in Ashland, MA

What the housing in Ashland is actually like

The stock here is older housing near the centre and the rail line, a body of post-war capes and ranches, and considerable development from the 1980s onward.

a wide age spread in a compact town, so the assessment matters more than a standard scope. The older centre housing has retrofitted systems; the newer stock is hitting its first renovation cycle.

What we do in Ashland

  • Kitchens. the post-1980 colonials have the standard closed kitchen and unused formal dining room. Removing that wall is the common project.
  • Bathrooms. municipal services across most of the developed town, which keeps bathroom work uncomplicated.
  • Structural and envelope repair. Rot, sills, sheathing and the water problems behind them — the work that has to happen before finishes are worth paying for.
  • Decks, carpentry and painting. Including the finish carpentry that makes a renovated room look finished rather than merely new.

What moves the price on a Ashland project

A phone quote is a guess. We come out, measure, look behind the surfaces where we can, and send a written scope with a line-by-line price, a schedule, and a clear statement of what is not included. If we think the budget is wrong for the work, we say so before anything is signed. And when an older house hides something — which in Ashland it often does — you hear about it the same day, with what it changes and what it costs, before we carry on.
