## Paints and stains we use

We choose the product by the surface, not by habit. These are the brands we buy and apply most often, and we can source others on request.

- **Benjamin Moore.** Regal Select for walls and ceilings; ADVANCE, a waterborne alkyd, for trim, doors and cabinets that need a hard, smooth finish.
- **Sherwin-Williams.** Emerald Interior for washable walls; Emerald Urethane Trim Enamel for trim, doors and cabinets, inside and out.
- **C2 Paint.** LUXE for walls and Cabinet & Trim for woodwork, across one full color palette.
- **Fine Paints of Europe.** Hollandlac, a high-gloss oil enamel from Holland, for front doors and period woodwork; Eurolux for walls. A premium option when finish quality matters most.
- **Farrow & Ball.** Water-based finishes known for depth of color: Estate Emulsion for low-traffic walls, Modern Emulsion for kitchens and baths, Flat Eggshell for trim. A premium option.
- **Cabot.** Penetrating and solid stains for decks, fences and siding, including Australian Timber Oil.
- **ProLuxe (formerly Sikkens).** Premium penetrating finishes for decks, railings and siding.

Manufacturer warranties vary by product and require installation to the manufacturer's instructions.
