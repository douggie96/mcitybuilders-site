Gallery intro: Carpentry ideas for Central Massachusetts homes, from mudroom lockers to stair treads.

- built-ins-and-mudrooms: Bookcases, window seats, office walls and mudroom lockers, built to fit the room you actually have.
- trim-crown-and-wainscoting: Crown, baseboard, casing and wall paneling that fit tight on real walls, from 1790s plaster to new drywall.
- interior-doors-and-closets: Solid-core doors that close quietly, old openings that finally latch, and closets that hold what you own.
- stairs-and-railings: Carpet off, oak treads on, new balusters and a solid newel. Stairs that look finished and feel steady.
