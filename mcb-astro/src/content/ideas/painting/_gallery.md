Ideas for the painting jobs that sit between a full repaint and a remodel: woodwork, wall repairs, decks and fences, and old wallpaper.

- trim-door-and-stair-painting: Baseboards, casings, doors and stairs in a hard enamel finish: the prep, the products and typical costs.
- drywall-repair-and-painting: Holes, nail pops, cracks and ceiling stains patched, primed and painted so the repair disappears.
- deck-and-fence-staining: Clean, brighten and stain wood decks and fences, with the right opacity and timing for a New England winter.
- wallpaper-removal: Old wallpaper stripped, adhesive washed off and walls repaired and sealed so fresh paint lies flat.
