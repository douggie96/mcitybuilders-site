Intro: Decks in Central Massachusetts have to handle deep frost, heavy snow and humid summers, so browse these deck ideas for typical costs, materials and code points by deck type.

- pressure-treated-decks — The most affordable real-wood deck. Built right, with the right lumber and hardware, it lasts for decades.
- pvc-decks — All-plastic boards with no wood fiber. The low-upkeep pick for shaded, damp lots and pool decks.
- deck-railings-and-stairs — The part of a deck you touch and the part inspectors check hardest. Wood, composite, aluminum or cable.
- deck-repair-and-rebuilds — Soft boards, a wobbly rail or a ledger with no flashing? Find out whether to repair, resurface or rebuild.
- multi-level-decks-and-pergolas — Two tiers down a sloped yard, an open-top pergola for shade, and built-in benches and planters.
