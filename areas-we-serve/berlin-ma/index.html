ABOUT BERLIN — WHAT THE HOUSING STOCK LOOKS LIKE

Berlin is a small rural Worcester County town of roughly 3,000 people, tucked between Route 62 and I-495 and bordered by Bolton to the north, Clinton to the west, Hudson to the east, and Marlborough to the southeast. It is about 15 minutes from our Lancaster base, which makes it one of the towns we reach most often. Despite its size, Berlin carries a wide range of housing stock, and the right approach to a remodel here depends heavily on which part of town a home sits in and how old it is.

The town's oldest homes are the antique 18th- and 19th-century colonials and farmhouses that line Central Street, South Street, and Linden Street near the town center. These are the projects that call for period-correct trim, plaster repair, and careful attention to original framing — you cannot treat a pre-1850 farmhouse the way you would treat a builder-grade colonial. Away from the center, newer subdivisions and custom homes sit on larger wooded lots, many of them built from the 1980s onward, where open-concept kitchens, updated baths, and composite decks are the typical scope.

Berlin is predominantly private well and septic country rather than municipal sewer, and that single fact shapes almost every kitchen, bathroom, and deck project in town. Septic tank and leach-field siting, plus the required setbacks, dictate where drain lines can run and where deck footings can safely go. We identify the septic system, the leach field, and the well during the estimate walk-through and build those constraints into the plan up front, so nothing gets discovered mid-project. Well-water pressure also factors into how we spec fixtures and valves for a bath or kitchen.

The commercial side of Berlin is anchored by Highland Commons, the retail development at the I-495 and Route 62 interchange, but the town remains overwhelmingly residential and rural in character. Permits for remodeling in Berlin are filed through the Berlin Building Department at the Berlin Town Offices, 23 Linden Street. Standard residential permits for kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, and deck construction are typically reviewed within a few weeks, and MCB prepares every application and schedules the inspections so Berlin homeowners never have to manage that process themselves.
